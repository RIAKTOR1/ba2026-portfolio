### Field 1 · Concept

In an AI-enabled economy, durable graduate employability rests on the **Four Pillars of Durable Capabilities**:

1. **Human Capabilities:** Interpersonal empathy, high-stakes negotiation, and trust-based relationship management with B2B counterparties where informal reciprocity governs transactions.
2. **Business Capabilities:** Applied financial literacy, command of national tax regimes, disciplined gross-margin defense, and non-delegable fiduciary accountability before regulators and owners.
3. **Digital Capabilities:** Architecting and governing domain software workflows (POS/ERP), verifying algorithmic pipelines, and orchestrating real-time multi-node database synchronization.
4. **Meta-Capabilities:** Epistemic vigilance, critical auditing of algorithmic blind spots, adaptive judgment under supply uncertainty, and autonomous physical quality verification.

**Concept Check:** As generative and analytical software drives the marginal cost of routine calculation and clerical lookup toward zero, pure computational execution loses market scarcity. Automation therefore shifts specialist value upward toward **meta-capabilities and local contextual interpretation**—auditing *when* standard models misfire against informal credit norms, physical product defects, or statutory constraints.
